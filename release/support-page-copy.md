## Common Help Topics

### I cannot sign in

Use the "Forgot Password" option on the onboarding screen to receive a password reset email.

### I am not receiving notifications

Confirm notifications are enabled in your device settings. Also make sure you are assigned to a service or role that should receive the reminder or fill-in request.

### I need to update my unavailable dates

Open Profile, select the dates you are unavailable, and tap Save.

### I want to delete my account

Open Profile and choose Delete Account. This removes your account and related app data where possible.

### I need help with church roles, schedules, or members

Contact your church admin. Admin users can manage members, roles, recurring services, reminder settings, and song types from the Church tab.
